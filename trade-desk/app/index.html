SIGNAL POSITIONS

Exit plan

EasyEquities supports Take Profit and Stop Loss only on qualifying instruments. Its published rules say only one SELL order per share can be active at a time, so Trade Desk recommends protecting downside first with a Stop Loss. If you switch to Take Profit, confirm that you cancelled or updated the existing sell order. Advanced orders can carry an additional order-admin fee, so the signal engine is instructed to allow for normal costs and spread before issuing BUY.
